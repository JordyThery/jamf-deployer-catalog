# Mac Preferences and Privacy

Everyday settings and privacy approvals.

- **Finder** preferences for every Mac.
- **Energy Saver** for laptops and desktops, plus a wake and sleep schedule
  for shared desktops.
- **Managed login items**: allows the background services of managed apps
  (macOS 13 and later).
- **Privacy approvals** for Jamf Self Service+, and for a management helper
  at `/usr/local/etc/management_tools/warden` when a Mac has it, on Macs
  with User Approved MDM.
- **Privilege elevation**: lets users on Macs with Administrator Rights set
  to Assigned request temporary administrator rights through Self Service+.
